## 0. Field semantics for consumers

- `day` = days since 2009-04-01 (4/7 = 6, 1/31 = 305); `wd` is the ISO weekday. Slot `day` is the after-school slot on school days and the daytime otherwise; `eve` is the evening.
- `dayLock`/`eveLock`: reason id (see `lockReasons`) or null. A null slot is free; nothing else is implied.
- `lockReasons.christmas_date` (平安夜约会) is used by no day record: when the player has accepted a Christmas Eve invitation (Profile.christmasLink) the engine treats 12/24 daytime and evening as taken with this reason.
- `sources[].edition`: `reload`, `game_data_reload` or `cn_guide_reload` (Reload sources) or `reload+portable` / `mixed` (corroboration only); every record's `src` names at least one Reload source (§1.1).
- `moon` is the decorative phase (0 new … 4 full … 7); `fullMoon` marks the operation full moons (4/9 … 11/3) and the finale on 1/31; the estimated full moons 12/2 and 1/1 only show phase 4.
- `tartarus` (per day): an optional Tartarus visit is possible that evening (block open, not a closed night, evening not locked). `block` is the block index for the day, -1 outside the plannable blocks.
- `avail`: days with an ordinary meeting cell (already filtered by locks). `invite`: holiday invitation days from game data; accepting uses the daytime slot and counts as a meeting. Both require the link to be established (rank >= 1) and the person not to be missing.
- `threshold[r]` = points needed while at rank r-1 to reach rank r (0 means the next meeting ranks up). `eventGain[r]` = best-answer points of the rank-r scene, counted toward rank r+1.
- `unlock`: `stat`/`link` requirements apply to every step. Steps run in order; `minGapDays` is measured from the previous step's day (0 = same day allowed). `consumesSlot=false` steps need a free slot of their kind on an allowed day but leave that slot usable for another action; they must still respect `minGapDays`. `days`: fixed = `fixedDays` (the ordinary meeting days of the link whose place the step uses, never its holiday invitations; Hierophant's opening only on school days), weekdays, any = any free slot; the builder no longer emits avail. The last step (`establishes`) sets rank 1. No steps = automatic: rank 1 once `earliestDay` is reached and the requirements hold (Magician on 4/22; Strength when Chariot reaches R2; story links by date).
- `missing`: the person is gone from `fromDay` to `toDay` unless the matching rescue (`tartarus.rescues`) happened earlier; without a rescue by `toDay` the link is lost for the rest of the game.
- `storyRanks` (story links) list dated rank events; the loader turns them into `storyRankDay[r]` = first day with rank >= r (skipped ranks share the next event's day).
- `activities`: `slots`, `weekdays`, `dayTypes` (empty = all), `fromDay..toDay`, `cost` (yen), `gain` (hidden stat points), `requires` (stat Lv). Jobs pay `pay` = [[minShiftNumber, minimum pay], …] for the N-th completed shift.
- `episodes` (Link Episodes, 羁绊剧情): opt-in tracks. `parts[n-1].days` = days on which episode n can be watched (slot kind = `evening`), in order, one slot each; `gain` = Academics/Charm/Courage points of that episode; no Social Link points. The loader turns the parts into `dayMask[day]` (bit n-1 = episode n allowed).
- `datingNote` (结缘网站的字条): from `fromDay`, an evening (club + dorm PC) that costs `cost` yen and adds `gain` points (no multipliers) to one established manual link at rank 1-9; it never ranks the link up by itself. `requires` = stat Lv to enter the club; `maxUses` = null means no limit.
- `conf`: game_data > verified > single > estimate; conflict = strictest claim chosen, alternatives in `alt` (activities) or in this report.

## 1. Sources and the Reload-only source policy

### 1.1 Reload-only source policy

The pack describes Persona 3 Reload (Xrd777 main game) only. Every source names the edition it describes (`sources[].edition` in the pack, `edition` in `rules/sources/SOURCES.json`):

- `reload` — Reload source: about Persona 3 Reload only (official Steam text, Reload forum threads, Reload-only guides and community pages, evidence files written for Reload).
- `game_data_reload` — Reload source: tables extracted from the Persona 3 Reload (Xrd777) game files.
- `cn_guide_reload` — Reload source: Simplified Chinese walkthrough of Persona 3 Reload, or evidence derived from it.
- `reload+portable` — not a Reload source: states the fact for Reload and Portable alike; corroboration only.
- `mixed` — not a Reload source: multi-edition page or post (P3/FES/Portable/Reload) of which only the Reload section is used; corroboration only.

Rules, enforced by the builder (the build fails otherwise):

1. Every record that carries `src` (days, links, unlock paths, story ranks, missing windows, activities, episodes, blocks, rescues, stats, policy, dating-site note) cites at least one Reload source. `RulesLoader` checks the same rule again when the app loads the pack.
2. The values below rest on the sources listed, all of which the holding record cites; at least one of them is a Reload source. A `mixed` or `reload+portable` source only corroborates.
3. Material that the evidence files tied to FES or Portable is never cited (§2 scope); multi-edition pages are used only for their Reload section and are marked `mixed`.
4. Snapshot URLs are checked against the snapshot itself: Steam threads point to `https://steamcommunity.com/app/2161700/discussions/0/<id>/` and must be Reload General Discussions threads, the store page to `https://store.steampowered.com/app/2161700/`, achievement pages to their language, the 3DM page to its pager entry.

## 2. Decisions

### scope

- **editions** — Only the main game (Xrd777) is described. Astrea/Episode Aigis tables are not used; FES/Portable material that the evidence files already rejected stays out (gorillawiki entry 103, the FES July calendar, atwiki page 36, P3P rank tables, samurai-gamers reversal mechanics, the Persona 4 '+10' paragraph, megatenwiki's Reload calendar with wrong full moons). Reload has no female-protagonist route, so no such links exist. Every source carries an edition and no record or listed value rests on non-Reload sources alone (§1.1).
- **precedence** — Evidence order: game-data tables > aqiu calendar cells cross-checked with the atwiki tables and the CN guide > single reports. When sources conflict the strictest Reload claim is used (fewer meeting days, higher requirements, lower gains) and the conflict is listed in section 3.

Days after a full moon are free when aqiu has cells for them (5/10, 6/9, 7/8, 8/7, 9/6); 10/5 daytime and 11/4 stay locked.
- **12-24 free** — Christmas Eve stays free by default (all invitations declined, DESIGN §10.5); aqiu has ordinary cells that day and wiki420 says both slots are free when every invitation is refused. A player who accepted an invitation records the link in Profile.christmasLink; the engine then treats 12/24 daytime and evening as taken with the lock reason `christmas_date` (平安夜约会). No day record carries that lock because it depends on the player's choice, not on the calendar.
- **day types** — school/sunday/holiday/vacation/exam/story with precedence exam > story (both slots locked) > vacation > holiday > sunday > school. Summer vacation 7/26–8/31 and winter break 12/27–1/7 follow the aqiu cells: school links still meet on 7/24–7/25 (exam results, Lovers opening in the classroom on 7/25) and on 12/24–12/26, and resume on 1/8 (class answer that day). Holidays are the 2009-10 Japanese public holidays; every weekday Hermit cell falls on one of them (audited). The 5/6 substitute holiday is not observed in-game (class answer and school-link cells on 5/6).
- **moon** — Moon phase 0..7 (4 = full, only on the full-moon day itself) is interpolated between the operation full moons 4/9 … 11/3 and 1/31, with two estimated full moons 12/2 and 1/1 (real 2009 dates) because the game has no operations in between. Decorative only; fullMoon is true only on operation days and 1/31.

### tartarus

- **blocks** — Block opening dates and floor ranges come from ActivityDataAsset (4/21, 5/10, 6/13, 7/9, 8/8, 9/10, 10/6, 11/6, 12/3, 1/1). The 1/31 row (257F+) is the finale and is not a plannable block. zh names come from the 3DM SC guide (single source); the two halves of a zone share its name and are told apart by floor range.
- **rescues** — Only the two missing people that gate a Social Link are rescues: 北村文吉 9/12–10/3 (教皇) and 舞子 10/21–11/2 (倒悬者) (aqiu overworld; the game board text says Bunkichi was reported missing on 9/11, but the aqiu calendar still has a Hierophant cell that day). The other 19 missing people only reward items and are left out.
- **closed nights** — A night is closed to Tartarus when the GameFAQs closure list (20 dates, both evidence files agree) or aqiu's nighttime 'Tartarus' column says so. That column names the party members who cannot go; 'Everyone' means nobody can, so there is no trip (every GameFAQs date is 'Everyone'; no wiki420 or CN-guide Tartarus night is; 10/4 'Ken, Shinjiro', 11/6-11/13 'Mitsuru' and 12/10-12/29 'Aigis' match story absences; a named member means a partial party and keeps the tower open). Nights closed by aqiu alone (conf=single): 04-29, 05-05, 05-25, 05-30, 06-01, 06-09, 06-10, 06-11, 06-12, 06-21, 06-28, 07-08, 07-09, 07-10, 07-13, 09-06, 09-07, 09-08, 09-09, 10-05, 10-06, 10-12, 11-05.

### links

- **eventGain** — eventGain[r] = best-answer points of the rank-r scene (they count toward r+1), lowest of aqiu's reported optimum and the normalised model; ranks 0-1 and 10 are 0, rank 2 never matters because every link needs 0 points for ranks 1-3 (game data). Romantic and platonic rank-9 scenes give the same points for every romanceable link, so eventGainRomance is null everywhere.
- **storyRanks** — Fool and Death ranks are dated story events (aqiu, matching the evidence file; skipped ranks are reached on the next event's day). Judgement R1 is 12/31; R2-R10 depend on Adamah floors 227-255, so they carry the conservative date 1/30 (last Tartarus evening) with conf=estimate.
- **invites** — Invitations come from HolidayScheduleDataAsset: 35 dates, 77 link-invitations. All 75 aqiu D cells are in the table; the table adds Lovers on 8/3 and 8/9 (resolving the old aqiu-internal discrepancy in favour of game data).
- **names** — Arcana and person names come from the zh-Hans game tables (DatRaceDataAsset, DatPlayerNameDataAsset): 女皇, 倒悬者, 月亮, 贝培, Ｙ子, 舞子 (no surname in-game), 北村文吉、光子. Judgement shows the spoiler-safe 伙伴们 instead of the official label 倪克斯讨伐队; Fool uses the official 特别课外活动部.
- **avail** — avail = aqiu O cells minus locked slots, minus Empress Monday/Friday cells outside the pre-exam week 12/7-12/12 (DESIGN conflict policy), minus Emperor cells 11/3-11/29 (aqiu note; one contrary cell on 11/16 dropped as the stricter claim). Cells inside rescue windows are kept: the missing window, not the calendar, decides whether the person is there.
- **empress exam** — aqiu requires Academics Lv6 and first place in one exam for Empress Rank 1 (the CN guide only says 学力MAX, an omission). Exam placement cannot be forecast and is not part of the player's recorded progress, so the pack models Academics Lv6 only and tells the player instead: the unlock note, the step how-to and the 【女皇】 pack note all say a first place is needed and that the goal should be set to 未选 when the player never placed first. This is a deliberate exception to the strictest-claim rule: modelling it as unmet would drop Empress from every plan, and as met it is only wrong for players the note addresses.
- **holds** — No mid-chain holds are encoded. Moon R9: aqiu says the link 'will momentarily reverse'; the CN guide lists only one later meeting (MAX on 11/23) and no repair step, so the reversal is treated as part of the scene and the 15-point R10 threshold already asks for extra meetings. Hierophant R4->R5: a single Steam report of a rumour gate is contradicted by the CN guide (R4 on 5/14, R5 on 5/15). Both are documented in noteZh/notes; players can still report a hold.
- **unlock step days** — Unlock steps are emitted with explicit day lists (days=fixed): the ordinary meeting days (aqiu O cells after filtering) of the link whose place the step uses, never the holiday-invitation days (an invitation needs an established link, and on Sundays/vacations the school is closed). hierophant.open is limited to school days: the persimmon leaf is in the school corridor (aqiu Rank 1 steps, CN guide 4/25 after school), so Sundays, holidays and vacation days do not work for that step even though the bookstore meetings do.

### activities

- **selection** — Planned activities: desk study (evening; daytime on days without school), library (school days, +2 lower bound), Wakatsu/Hagakure/Wilduck regular meals, karaoke, the three arcade games (evening only, the stricter reading), the three weekday movie programmes, Chagall coffee, and the three jobs. Every row is checked against the aqiu table (gains never above aqiu, same price, slots a subset) and, where present, the evidence files and the game's job help text.
- **excluded** — Not planned: Wakatsu seafood course, Hagakure bowl and the weekend Wilduck set (their unlock needs an observed menu event or Request #11 that the model cannot express), the one-off burger challenge (needs Requests #1/#9/#11), shrine donations (single source, unclear Reload value), dorm hangouts (DESIGN §10.10), film-festival hangouts and shrine fortunes. Link Episodes are opt-in tracks (`episodes`) and the dating-site note is `datingNote`, both outside `activities`.
- **no idle slots** — Every unlocked slot from 4/21 offers at least one free productive activity (evening desk study; library after school; desk study in the daytime when there is no school), so a plan never has to leave a slot idle; jobs are the only forecast income and beat idling whenever money is useful. The builder fails if a free slot lacks such a fallback. Reload has no fatigue or sickness, so a Tartarus night carries no next-day penalty.
- **jobs** — Jobs pay the conservative non-busy tier per completed shift (wage tables from the Persona 3 Reload sections of the MegaTen Wiki pages Chagall_Cafe (café), Be_Blue_V (accessory shop) and Port_Island_Station (Screen Shot cinema), and wiki469; base pay confirmed by the game help text: café ¥2,500+ at night, accessory shop ¥3,500+ by day, cinema ¥5,000+ by day). Chagall from 5/4 (first CN-guide witness; earliest date otherwise unknown), accessory shop Mon–Fri from 7/24, cinema on Sundays. Where sources disagree on the second stat only Academics +1 is credited.

### episodes

- **tracks** — Link Episodes (羁绊剧情) are opt-in tracks for Junpei, Akihiko, Koromaru, Ken and Shinjiro, parsed from the aqiu calendar columns Jun/Kor/Shi (daytime) and Aki/Ken (nighttime). A cell names the episode numbers that can be watched that day ('3' or '1-4'); each episode must also fall inside its window in the aqiu Linked Episodes table, and locked slots are dropped. Episodes run in order, take one slot of the track's kind and give no Social Link points; `gain` holds the Academics/Charm/Courage points of the table (other rewards are only described). Every CN-guide witness date (5/12, 8/9, 11/7, 12/19, 1/15 Junpei; 5/29, 7/24, 10/9, 12/12, 1/8 Akihiko; 9/9, 9/16, 11/10, 12/22, 1/19 Ken; 9/11, 9/13, 9/21, 9/24 Shinjiro; 1/19 Koromaru) is an allowed day.
- **excluded** — Ryoji's column is not a track: his availability is 'Preset', episode 1 (11/9) and episode 5 (12/31) are automatic story scenes, and the slots in between are fixed dates around the story. Takaya's episodes are automatic or a single club meeting and are not in the calendar. Shinjiro's episode 5 (his desk drawer from 10/5, the CN guide does it on 10/28) is not a calendar slot, so his track has 4 plannable episodes; the note explains the drawer. Nothing here claims that an episode changes Shinjiro's story: in Reload it does not.
- **school days** — Shinjiro episodes 1 and 4 start with Mitsuru in the faculty hallway (aqiu steps; CN guide 9/11 and 9/24 after school), so they are kept on school days only.

### datingNote

- **rule** — 结缘网站的字条 (Dating Site Note): bought from the URL seller in Club Escapade for ¥3,000 and used the same evening on the dorm's shared PC; +10 points to one established, non-MAX, non-reversed manual link (aqiu overworld, game8, affinity_actions_candidate.json); it never ranks the link up by itself. Points are added without multipliers (conf=estimate: the Persona/Charm bonus on notes is not proven). The club needs Courage Lv2 at night (siliconera; the CN guide notes the club opens at night from Courage Lv2).
- **sale date** — From 11/11: aqiu overworld, game8 and the Steam guide say 11/11 and the CN guide uses a note on the evening of 11/11 for 山岸风花 (a play witness). atwiki 455 says 11/16; that stricter claim is outvoted by the witness.
- **repeatable** — maxUses = null (no limit): siliconera ('can be purchased multiple times'), atwiki 455 ('infinitely purchasable', via ordinary_hangout_rules_candidate.json) and PC Invasion agree that the note can be bought again; each use still takes an evening.

### policy

- **ordinaryGain** — Ordinary (non-rank-up) meeting = +10 points: a single Reload playthrough report, consistent with the two notes shown for a normal hangout at 5 points per note (atwiki 455). conf=estimate. Multipliers 1.51 (matching Persona), 1.51 (Charm Lv6), 1.51/1.21 (exam) are game data; rounding is unknown, so the engine floors per meeting.

## 6. Known gaps

- Ordinary (non-rank-up) hangout gain +10 rests on one playthrough report (the Reload subsection of a multi-edition post, edition mixed) and its Reload-scoped evidence file (conf=estimate). The rank-up cue the game shows on the map is the reliable signal; the engine/UI should prefer it over the estimate.
- Multiplier rounding order (per answer vs per meeting) and the exam-bonus expiry are unknown; the pack says floor_per_meeting.
- Free stat gains (class answers, exam top-of-class Charm +4, summer school Academics +18, Link Episodes, dorm hangouts, nurse's office) are not forecast (DESIGN §10.9); the player syncs stats.
- Not modelled: Wakatsu seafood course, Hagakure bowl, weekend Wilduck set, burger challenge, shrine donations, shrine fortunes, gifts, film-festival hangouts, Koromaru walks, Ryoji's and Takaya's Link Episodes. All of them can only add points/stats, so plans stay conservative.
- Dating-site note: +10 points is the reported base; whether the Persona/Charm multipliers apply is unknown (the pack applies none). The 11/11 sale date rests on aqiu/game8/Steam plus the CN-guide witness (atwiki: 11/16).
- Link Episodes: the aqiu calendar has a few cells outside the episode's own window (Junpei #3 on 11/13, Akihiko #4 on 12/26); they are dropped. Shinjiro's episode 5 (desk drawer from 10/5) is not a slot and is not a part.
- No official zh-Hans names were found for the library, karaoke venue, Be Blue V (shown as 饰品店) and the Screen Shot cinema (shown as 电影院), or for stat level titles (UI shows Lv.N).
- Judgement R2-R10 depend on Adamah floors 227-255, not on dates; the pack dates them 1/30 (conf=estimate).
- Empress: aqiu adds 'first place in one exam' to Academics Lv6. Not modelled (cannot be forecast); the unlock note, the step how-to and the 【女皇】 pack note tell the player to set the goal to 未选 without a first place.
- Mid-chain holds: none encoded. Moon R9 'momentary reversal' and the single Steam report of a Hierophant R4->R5 rumour gate are documented only; the result sheet's 'temporarily cannot rank up' outcome covers both.
- Item preconditions are not modelled as inventory: Odd Morsel (神秘食品, random Tartarus chest) for Moon, Weird Takoyaki + Mad Bull for Hanged Man (their 510 yen is charged on the gift step), the red fountain pen for Sun.
- Devil payments 1-2, Justice talks 1-3, the Moon/Tower rumours and Sun's pen steps are marked consumesSlot=false because the CN guide does another action in the same slot; Sun's pen retrieval timing is not proven.
- Unresolved story days are locked conservatively (Yakushima 7/20-7/23, 11/4, 1/1 daytime, 1/21 daytime, track training daytimes 7/27-8/2). Owner verification in the game can free them.
- Christmas Eve: 12/24 is free by default. The pack does not list who can invite the player that day; an accepted invitation is the player's input (Profile.christmasLink), and the engine then locks both slots with `christmas_date`. The summer-festival partner choice is not modelled (8/16 locked).
- 6/13 evening is locked as a mandatory story Tartarus night and does not count as a block visit.
- Missing-person floors are not in the data; the pack only knows the rescue windows.
- The analysis report mentioned 34 invitation dates; HolidayScheduleDataAsset has 35 dates (77 link-invitations).
- Several evenings lack the usual Devil/Tower cell (see audit list) but are kept free because nothing says other evening activities are unavailable.
- Tartarus closures: 20 GameFAQs dates plus the aqiu 'Everyone' nights (conf=single). Nights where aqiu names only some party members stay open (partial party).
- Almost every numeric calendar fact traces back to the aqiu/marendarade lineage; 'verified' means the atwiki weekday tables or the CN guide agree, not that the game was replayed.
- Tartarus income is not forecast (DESIGN §10.4); jobs are the only forecast income.
