# P3R source-rule package 0.5.0

This package extends 0.4.0 without changing its immutable snapshot or the original daily guide. Community sources were cross-checked; game replay, inspected game code, and verified Simplified Chinese option order are not claimed. Consumer engine/UI support must be tested separately.

## Introduction and prerequisite planning

- `simple_unlock_rules_candidate.json`: 11 direct or one-slot compound introductions plus the forced Magician milestone. `multistep_unlock_rules_candidate.json`: seven staged introduction chains, typed guards/effects and explicit conflicts. Together these cover procedures for all 19 manual links, not a claim every prerequisite dispute is resolved.
- Use introduction guards, not the ordinary-visit `alreadyUnlocked=true` guard. A later observed introduction can override an uncertain earliest-date claim, never an unrelated prerequisite.
- Multi-location preparations within a source-witnessed one-slot compound action do not each consume a day. Conversely, unknown standalone step costs remain unknown. Justice preparation requires distinct dates; Devil payments cost 20,000/10,000/10,000 yen and slot costs 0/0/1.
- Current preparation state may be known even without history. For a known prior step with unknown date, schedule its next distinct-day step after the current checkpoint date. Unknown preparation state is not “not started”; do not repay or consume items again by default.
- Devil Hermit rank, Tower Courage rank, Sun opening date and Empress extra exam gate retain explicit competing claims. Conservative sufficient gates are opt-in assumptions where indicated; lower values do not prove impossibility.
- `unlock_compound_alternatives.json` provides a source-witnessed complete Sun preparation-plus-introduction alternative, not invented costs for its isolated partial steps.

## Stats and affinity

- `stat_activity_expansion_candidate.json` adds 11 repeatable actions and one challenge to the existing five. Menu unlocks, exact-date/slot availability, money and challenge outcomes remain guards.
- `stat_lower_bound_adapter_contract.json` permits base-only lower bounds when the temporary Temperance shuffle-card bonus is unknown and sourced as nonnegative. This is not the Temperance social link. It does not turn rank-only input into an exact raw stat value.
- `ordinary_hangout_rules_candidate.json` supports choice-free filler interaction, affinity increase, next-meeting rank-up timing and no surplus carry through a rank event. Exact ordinary gain remains disabled pending stronger numeric evidence.
- `observation_meeting_policy.json` offers a guarded meeting checkpoint when readiness is unknown. First inspect whether the game offers rank-up or filler; do not predict rankDelta 0 across an unknown branch. Confirmed filler consumes one slot, does not rank up in that slot, and has no invented numeric gain. Repeat only after new observation; no fixed visit count or guaranteed MAX finish date.
- `affinity_actions_candidate.json` supplies two late-game +10 base-lower-bound actions: use an owned 结缘网站的字条, or buy and use one for 3,000 yen. The owned-item path is not charged again. Target menu eligibility and PC/night availability are explicit. Conservative sale start November 16 retains the November 11 evidence conflict; observed inventory/availability is usable.
- Existing rank-event models replace current-rank lower-bound knowledge after a completed rank-up; do not carry surplus. Unknown actual choices cannot inherit a forecast maximum. An insufficient lower bound means unknown readiness, not false readiness.

## Story and tower

- `story_link_transitions_candidate.json` has 25 milestone transitions, including skipped ranks. A date alone never awards a rank. Confirm the completed event; mandatory events consume no additional optional slot. Highest floor alone does not establish a boss victory or rescue.
- `stage_identification_contract.json` supersedes raw date claims in `tower_stages.json` for operational cap accounting. Ten conservative date interiors are supported. June 13, July 9–17 and December 3–9 retain manual/current-block resolution; optional starts are June 14, July 18 and December 10. Evidence and competing nominal dates are preserved in `tower_boundary_semantics_evidence.json`.
- Stage identity is not nightly entry permission. Every stable stage keeps its configured cap and observed/unknown used visits. Unknown counts never become zero. Mandatory tutorial/final operations are distinct from optional exploration sessions. Do not silently migrate existing visits between revised boundaries.
- Known floor ceilings remain enforceable with a manually observed stage even when its date claim is contested. If a reported floor exceeds that ceiling, request reconciliation instead of clamping the observation.

## Branches and Chinese wording

- A reversal-producing choice interrupts a predicted completed rank transition: request actual resulting rank and relationship state. Do not automatically award the target rank or assume the old rank survived.
- Romance eligibility may follow an explicitly selected modeled choice. Friendship/romantic relationship type requires a declared route decision; unknown entry eligibility blocks branch selection. Relationship type and normal/reversed status are separate.
- Moon rank 9 applies only after the complete event, not an intermediate reversal state.
- `guide_dialogue_reference_map.json` preserves 79 literal guide references. They are display references, not verified in-game Simplified Chinese option ordering or point bindings. No source ordinal may be displayed as an official game answer number.

## Validation and limitations

Five independent data validators cover source-model graphs, 153 incoming thresholds, 897 answer values, calendar candidates, preparation slot/resource semantics, stage gaps and baseline preservation. These are data checks, not proof of all-goal executable planning. See `missing_rules_ledger.json` for researched unknowns, available but unmapped rules and intentional observed outcomes. A missing rule never proves a selected goal impossible.
