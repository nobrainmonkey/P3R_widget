Always save exploration until the countdown timer in the top right of the screen nears 0 aka the last day. That's the best strat I can suggest to use in avoiding missing out on saving everyone you can. Also, remember that when you hop in to Tartarus when you talk to Elizabeth she'll tell you what floor they're on, AND she will tell you if any lost kitties wandered in. Lost cats will usually be found on previous floors, but they seem to always give you twilight shards. So imo they're definitely worth it as well as the people.
