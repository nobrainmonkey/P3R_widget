I think I read somewhere the actual internal amount of points all activities give you and the arcade games are indeed the maximum amount you can get for a social stat, but they are equivalent to the secret menu items at the restaurants. So if you're in the early game and you don't have the stat requirement to get access to the secret menu, then sure, go for the arcade if it's available. But after that, it's much cheaper to eat for you boosts.

Not that it's entirely necessary to min/max at all. You have a ton of opportunities at free stat boosts and you'll probably be maxing everything out long before you actually need it. You might want to rush Charm and Courage to the end of summer vacation, but Academics isn't even needed until the very end of November and you have tons of study sessions to get it up there.

Arcade Games: 4 points

Regular menu items: 3 points (♪♪)

Karaoke, Café, or Study alone: 2 points (♪)
Part-Time Jobs: 1 point (♪) each to two stats


Daytime-Only Activities
Theater (includes Film Festival): 4 points (♪♪)
Large donation at shrine: 3 (♪♪), 4 (♪♪), or 5 (♪♪♪) points at random
Medium donation at shrine: 2 (♪), 3 (♪♪), or 4 (♪♪) points at random
Small donation at shrine: 2 (♪) or 3 (♪♪) points at random


Limited Activities:
Dorm PC Software: 4 points (♪♪), one time per software bundle
Study with party the week before exams: 4 points (♪♪)
Study with party the night before exams: 5 points (♪♪♪)

Bonus from Dorm Hangouts and Link Episodes: 2 points (♪)


One-Time/Scripted Stat Gains:
Big Eater Challenge request: 4 points (♪♪) to each stat
Charm from exam results: 1 (♪), 2 (♪), 3 (♪♪), or 4 (♪♪) points depending on result
Summer school: 3 points (♪♪) per day
Choices during class: 2 points (♪)

Samurai Helmet request: 2 points (♪) per visit to faculty office

Originally posted by Omallis:
Thank you so much for taking the time to post this. So 2 points amounts to 1 musical note? If arcade and special menu both give 4 points does that mean there's no reason do the arcade since the menu is less expensive and you'd save more?

They're on different schedules, so you can be more efficient by alternating.

Courage is Arcade on Tuesday and Friday, Special Menu and Saturday and Sunday
Charm is Arcade on Monday and Thursday, Special Menu on Tuesday, Wednesday, and Friday
Academics is Arcade on Wednesday and Saturday, Special Menu on Sunday, Monday, Thursday, and Friday

You still want to get them done in order (Courage, Charm, and Academics), and Reload adds a bunch of new nighttime activities, so there's even more value in being as efficient as possible with your social stat gains.
